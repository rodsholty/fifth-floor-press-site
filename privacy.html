Floor V · Unlisted

Privacy

A short note on the weekly list. Nothing here is sold.

Fifth Floor Press sends a weekly note: a free Kindle day, a new book, and nothing else. You only get it if you ask, and if you check the box that says so.

What we store

If you join, the list keeps your email address, your first name if you give one, the page you signed up from, the time you signed up, the fact that you agreed, and a token. The token is how the unsubscribe link in each note finds your row.

The list lives in a private Google Sheet controlled by Fifth Floor Press LLC. We do not sell it, and we do not pass it to anyone else for their own mail.

The hidden field

The form has a field people are not meant to see. If that field is filled in, we drop the submission. It is there for spam, not for you.

Leaving

Use the unsubscribe link in the note, open the unsubscribe page from that link, or email info@fifthfloorpressbooks.com. We mark the row unsubscribed and stop using the address.

Everything else

This site is static. Buying a book happens on Amazon, under Amazon’s own rules. The site does not run advertising trackers.
