Claire Dare

Crime fiction from the autopsy suite.

Claire Dare writes crime fiction about the dead and the people who speak for them. Her novels follow Dr. Nora Bishop, a forensic pathologist at the Bexar County Medical Examiner’s Office in San Antonio.

The Nora Bishop Novels

Settled

A Nora Bishop Novel, Book One

The letter was the twenty-first one.

Dana Whitlock Haynes was twenty-four when she was found face down in a Hill Country creek in April 2005. A justice of the peace with no medical training ruled it an accident in less than two days. Nobody ever autopsied her. Her mother never stopped writing letters. Dr. Nora Bishop has spent eleven years at the Bexar County Medical Examiner’s Office. Pregnant and benched from the autopsy table, she reads the twenty-first letter, looks at a set of old scene photographs, and sees what nobody in Medrano County wanted to see. So she starts a podcast from her closet. Then the county starts answering.
