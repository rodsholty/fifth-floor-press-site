About the Press

Fifth Floor Press publishes character-driven fiction across genres — medical drama, survival and techno-thriller, literary suspense, and crime. We work with a small roster of authors and put care into every title we put out, from the first draft to the finished page.

Our Authors

Owen Kessler, MD

Medical fiction grounded in clinical reality — author of the Triage Quartet.

R. J. Marek

Novels about systems and the people inside them — the Pioneer Mountain, Rally Point and Voice Register series, the Gil Prentice novels, and standalone fiction including Candid, Koshaku and Resignation.

Claire Dare

Crime fiction from the autopsy suite — author of the Nora Bishop novels.
