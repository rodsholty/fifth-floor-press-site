About the Press

Fifth Floor Press publishes character-driven fiction across genres — medical drama, post-apocalyptic survival, and techno-thriller. We work with a small roster of authors and put care into every title we put out, from the first draft to the finished page.

Our Authors

R. J. Marek

Post-apocalyptic and techno-thriller fiction — author of the Pioneer Mountain, Rally Point, and Voice series, and the Gil Prentice novels.
